Two-page spreads: `side="left"` on a `page` makes it a left-hand page, with a blank page before it when needed (also at the start of a chapter). The preview reports two or more pages of full art in a row whose first page is a right-hand page, so they do not face each other; the app lists the warning with the book's page numbers and marks the page in the editor.
