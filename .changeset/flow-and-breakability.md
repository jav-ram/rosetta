Flow and breakability: the Classic theme keeps unbreakable components, images, list items and table rows whole (driven by `data-breakable`), keeps headings with what follows, and takes orphans and widows from tokens. The preview repeats a table's header row when the table continues on a new page. A chapter of test pages (`theme-classic/test-pages/flow.md`) covers every case.
