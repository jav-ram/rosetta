Front cover in the PDF export: the first page, with the book's title (or the one set for the cover) over a picture chosen from the project's images, or on a plain color, followed by a blank page so that the first chapter is page 1 on a right-hand page. Neither is numbered or counted. Set it with Cover… in the editor's top bar. The book layout accepts a `cover`, and the exporter reads it from the manifest.
