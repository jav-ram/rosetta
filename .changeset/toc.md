`toc` component: `::toc{title="Contents" depth=2}` stands for a table of contents of the whole book, made from the headings of every chapter with the page number of each when the book is exported as a PDF. In the editor and a chapter's preview it is a labelled marker. The preview's book layout gets `addContents` and fills the page numbers after layout.
